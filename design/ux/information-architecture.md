# Information Architecture

**Status:** Draft

Parent and Child experiences are separate information architectures.

They share a common design language but serve different jobs.

## Child

Starting navigation hypothesis:

- Home
- Extras
- Activity
- Money

### Home

Primary operational surface.

It should quickly answer:

- What do I need to do?
- Is anything urgent?
- Do I have an active commitment?
- Are Extras available?
- What is my balance?

### Extras

Claimable chore discovery and commitment management.

### Activity

Household-visible activity without exposing sibling-private balances.

### Money

The Child's private running balance and relevant financial history.

## Parent

Starting navigation hypothesis:

- Home
- Chores
- Reviews
- Money
- Household

This remains a design hypothesis until reviewed.

Do not force Parent and Child into one shared navigation model.
