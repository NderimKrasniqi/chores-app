Direction

This pass follows the selected Concept E Child Home reference closely: a compact task-first screen with warm surfaces, bold rounded type, friendly artwork and a small encouragement moment.

Reconstruction focus

  • Illustrated Child greeting with a compact running-balance pill.
  • Green three-part segmented control.
  • Task-first hierarchy with four compact chore cards.
  • Coral urgency treatment for the most time-sensitive chore.
  • Inline status and real-SEK value chips on every card.
  • Friendly corgi progress banner and restrained sticker-like type.
  • Simple labeled bottom navigation.

Behavioral truth preserved

  • Personal responsibilities remain the primary Home content.
  • Values are shown as chore values, not fake game currency.
  • Dates and urgency are explicit rather than color-only.
  • No active Claimable commitment is invented for this reference state.
  • The running balance remains compact and separate from chore values.

Age-range check

The playful tone comes from the character and object artwork, color and one handwritten banner. Functional copy stays short and direct enough for both younger children and teens.
